`arches-timed-entry` and `zion-visitor-center` are taken as the Arches and Zion

**Verdict: the falsifier condition is met on the available evidence.** The
three masters did not out-impress the Arches master on reservation-phrased
queries (6 visible against 109), and their whole page-row total (135) is a
fifth of the Arches master's 647. Suppression means this is not exact: the
masters' ceiling is 135 and Arches' floor is 109, so a reversal is possible
but very unlikely. Against Zion the visible rows read 6 to 2, too few to mean
anything; the Zion page draws visitor-center searches, not reservation ones.
The Yosemite master is not indexed (below), so the hook was never tested for
the park the record expected to be largest; the condition is met for RMNP and
Glacier and untested for Yosemite. Lot pages carried the batch, as batches 4–5
predicted. Under the record's own words: the batch works as ordinary lot
coverage and the three-park contrast was not shown to be the hook. The
AI-citation check was not done.

  national-park season; RMNP timed entry ended in mid-October.
- The Adventure Pass guide (September 19) and its where-needed page lifted
  the whole site; no cohort page changed, but internal links and site-level
  signals did.
- September 30 autumn refresh and October 17 closures hub and Eaton-area
  corrections: none touched a cohort page.
- October 25 click-attribution deploy: no visible change, all sitemap
  `lastmod` values restamped, six days before the window ends.
- Cohorts are not age-matched (batch 6 is six days older than the window
  start) and Yosemite Valley and Glacier Point belong to both batch 6 and the
  famous cohort.
- Tinylytics counts include non-search and automated traffic.
