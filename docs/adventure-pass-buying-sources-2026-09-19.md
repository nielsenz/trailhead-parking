# Adventure Pass buying guide: source check, September 19, 2026

Published page: https://trailheadparking.com/adventure-pass/

## Primary sources

- [Forest Service Adventure Pass rules](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/passes/adventure-pass): $5 daily / $30 annual; annual validity; four-forest standard-amenity coverage; interagency acceptance; official vendor-map and online-seller links. Retrieved directly because the web reader returned 403. Do not equate a maintained trailhead with a designated fee site.
- [Forest Service vendor dashboard](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/dashboards/2d1b34fe80e948c5be01b3366d14119a): links to web map `4c80dab39a0043b0a21e39721781c3dc` and its public vendor layer below. Selected records are active and explicitly list Adventure Pass products. This is directory verification, not a telephone stock check.
- [Public vendor layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/gGHDlz6USftL5Pau/arcgis/rest/services/VendorLocations_PublicVIEWLayer/FeatureServer/2): queried all 1,139 records on September 19; no transfer-limit flag. Fields used: ObjectID, Business_Name, AddressCleaned, IsActive, ForestName, ForestProduct. See the adjacent JSON for the nine selected source records.
- [Big Bear Visitor Center](https://www.bigbear.com/travel-tools/visitor-center-big-bear-magazine/): operator confirms daily/annual sales, 40824 Big Bear Blvd, 909-866-7000, cash/card, published 9am–5pm daily hours. Availability and holiday hours are not guaranteed.
- [REI daily](https://www.rei.com/product/810590/southern-california-daily-forest-adventure-pass) and [REI annual](https://www.rei.com/product/810592/southern-california-annual-forest-adventure-pass): product listings at $5 and $30 with store-selection/pickup controls. No claim of universal branch stock.
- [MyScenicDrives daily](https://www.myscenicdrives.com/store/forest-passes/southern-california/daily), [annual](https://www.myscenicdrives.com/store/forest-passes/southern-california/annual), and [delivery FAQ](https://www.myscenicdrives.com/store/frequently-asked-questions): physical delivery, no printable forest pass or receipt substitute. Avoid promising an arrival date.
- [Recreation.gov San Bernardino day pass](https://www.recreation.gov/activitypass/AP21330): forest-specific digital product; selected vehicle/date, 11:59pm expiry, printed copy recommended in advance and plate-based compliance for on-site purchases. Its price was not visible in the readable page, so the guide directs users to checkout for the current price rather than assuming $5. Do not describe it as an Angeles/Cleveland/Los Padres pass.

## Editorial choices

Removed unverified claims about Nomad Ventures, Mountain Center Café, Bass Pro and library loans. Replaced generic chain-wide claims with named official listings. Kept America the Beautiful acceptance without repeating the former universal $80 annual-price claim. Removed the old assumption that most maintained trailhead lots need an Adventure Pass. No new page URLs; deep links identify sections of the existing guide.

Route grouping is editorial geography, not a statement that a road/trail is currently open. Seller records are not live inventory. Trailhead factual verification dates were preserved because only their related links changed.

Recheck vendor participation and product links before the next substantive update; hours and inventory should always be confirmed with the seller.
