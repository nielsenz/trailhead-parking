The Forest Service lists {{ fee_sites.total }} trailheads and day-use sites — picnic areas, OHV staging areas, vista points and similar — in the four Adventure Pass forests. Campgrounds, group sites and the Forest Service's target-range category aren't included; a few shooting areas filed as day-use areas, such as Munson Canyon, are. {{ fee_sites.counts.adventure_pass }} are listed as standard amenity fee sites, where a $5 daily or $30 annual Adventure Pass or a valid America the Beautiful pass covers the fee. {{ fee_sites.counts.no_fee }} list no fee.

The rest need a closer look: {{ fee_sites.counts.concession }} are run by concessionaires that may sell their own parking pass, {{ fee_sites.counts.conflicting }} have fee records that contradict each other, {{ fee_sites.counts.other_fee }} list a different fee type such as a special recreation permit, and {{ fee_sites.counts.fee_unspecified }} list a fee without saying what kind. Signs posted at the site are what count. Where we have checked a site ourselves, our verified answer appears next to the agency listing.
